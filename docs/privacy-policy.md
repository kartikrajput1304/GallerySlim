# GallerySlim privacy policy

_Draft for the Play Store listing. It needs review by a person before publishing._

GallerySlim makes the photos and videos on your phone smaller so they take less space. This page explains what it does with your data.

## Everything happens on your phone
Photos and videos are shrunk, checked and sorted on the phone itself. They are never uploaded to the developer or to anyone else.

## No account, no ads, no tracking
GallerySlim has no sign-in, shows no ads and contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code.

## Internet is used only for "Send to computer"
The app can use the network for one feature: sending photos to a computer on your own Wi-Fi, either to a browser page or to the GallerySlim Receiver program. Sharing runs while its notification is showing (Stop sharing ends it), sends only to the computer whose code you scanned and confirmed with your fingerprint or PIN (or, on a phone without a screen lock, with a confirmation message), and stops by itself 30 minutes after the last transfer or when Wi-Fi drops. Files go directly from the phone to that computer over your local network, never over the internet. Transfers to the Receiver are encrypted; the browser page is not.

## Nothing is deleted without you
Originals move to Android's trash only after you confirm, and stay restorable there for 30 days. Two features delete for good, each only after you confirm in the app and again in Android's own dialog: "Free this space now" (only originals that already have a verified smaller copy) and "Send the trash to computer" (only files the GallerySlim Receiver checked byte for byte on your computer).

## Text reading stays on the phone
Clean up reads text in photos to spot receipts and identity documents, using an on-device model bundled with the app. No image or text leaves the phone. Identity documents are never suggested for deletion.

## Permissions
- **Photos and videos**: to find and shrink them.
- **Camera**: only to scan the code shown on your computer for "Send to computer".
- **Fingerprint or PIN**: to confirm sharing, and for the optional app lock.
- **Notifications**: to show progress while shrinking or sharing.
- **Network and Wi-Fi multicast**: only for "Send to computer" on your local Wi-Fi.

## Your diagnostic log stays here
The app keeps a small log of what it did, on the phone. It is shared only if you tap "Share diagnostic log" yourself. App data (settings, history, search index) is excluded from Google backups and device-to-device transfers.
